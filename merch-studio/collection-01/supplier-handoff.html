Design and color system

The collection uses the existing Zebra Book Club crest, serif typography, book forms and simple monochrome graphics. Ink preview colors are near-black #111111 and warm cream #F4EFDF. These are screen references, not Pantone or thread codes. Match inks, patch yarns and threads to physical swatches before approval.

The vector masters are clean, standardized interpretations of the rendered designs. They control final geometry and lettering; the AI mockups communicate styling, placement and texture. Compare both at sample approval. The tracing preserves the hand-drawn character of the existing crest.

Printing and decoration

  • Screenprint: use SVG paths. Most designs use one visible ink; the supplier may require a white underbase on dark garments. Woven patches and the varsity appliqué use two visible colors. Knockout areas in the artwork should reveal the garment or patch ground. Ask for final separation, trapping and minimum-line review.
  • DTG or transfer alternative: use the transparent 300-DPI PNG at its stated physical size, or have the supplier rasterize the SVG to its own template. Confirm the accepted color space and whether the technique suits the chosen blank. Do not print the entire garment render board.
  • Embroidery: SVGs are artwork, not machine stitch files. The supplier must digitize for its machine, thread and cap/garment construction. Review tiny counters, serif tips, broad fills and center seams. Start with lettering at least about 6.35 mm tall and lines about 1.27 mm thick, then follow the supplier’s limits. Increase or simplify details where the sew-out requires it.
  • Raised embroidery: C03 uses raised ZBC letters with flat BOOK CLUB lettering. Confirm foam thickness, stitch-column widths and edge coverage. The flat artwork does not specify stitch count or stitch direction.
  • Woven patches and appliqué: the supplier sets the stitch border, cut line, seam allowance, backing and attachment. The original detailed crest is intended for a woven patch at small sizes, rather than direct embroidery. Approve a physical patch and an attached garment sample.

Reference constraints, not a selected supplier: Printful embroidery artwork guidance and print-file guidance. The logo format conversion uses Potrace; font outlines were created from locally installed Times New Roman and Arial. Font software is not distributed with this package.

Before a product goes on sale

  1. Shortlist designs and choose the supplier and exact blank SKUs.
  2. Get a quote for each decoration location, technique and color; confirm setup charges, minimums, shipping and lead time.
  3. Place the SVG/PNG files into that blank’s templates and approve the proof at actual size.
  4. Approve a physical print, patch or embroidery sample. Check readability, placement, fabric feel and wash durability.
  5. Confirm product composition, sizing, care instructions, fulfillment and returns; price from actual costs. Photograph the approved sample or clearly label render imagery before publishing listings.
