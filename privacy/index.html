This policy applies to zebrabookclub.com, the Zebra Book Club iOS app, and their shared book-based matchmaking platform, operated by Zebra Book Club ("we," "us," or "our"). For privacy questions or requests, contact zebrabookclub@proton.me.

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: your Google account identifier, email address, basic profile information such as your name and profile image, and authentication information needed to keep you signed in. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or Drive files.
  • Apple sign-in: your Apple account identifier, the name and email address Apple shares with us, and authentication information. You may choose an Apple private relay email address. We do not receive your Apple password. If you verify Apple when deleting your account, we send the authorization code to our server to remove Apple sign-in access; if automatic removal is unavailable, we delete your account data and explain how to remove that access yourself.
  • Email login: if your Zebra Book Club account has a password, Supabase processes your email address and password to authenticate you. Passwords are not included in your profile, shared with matches, or saved by the app. Password recovery sends a reset link to your account email; the app keeps the recovery verifier in its protected authentication storage.
  • Your private library: the books you save, optional ratings in quarter-star steps, and update times. Library entries and ratings are separate from matchmaking favorites and are not shown to other members.
  • Safety reports and blocks: the introduction and account identifiers involved, your chosen report reason and optional details, report review status and times, and blocks you create. Reports are private to authorized operators; they are not sent to the reported member.
  • Your matchmaking profile: your chosen name, optional Instagram username, birthday, city or nearby city, one to three favorite books, an optional current read, your reading preferences, an optional book you recommend to matches, and the connection types you select: romance, friends, business partners, or any combination of these.
  • Your preferences: where you would like to connect; for romance, your gender, the genders you would like to meet, your preferred age range, and an optional description of your ideal match; for friends or business partners, the introduction answers you choose to provide. Business profiles may also include an optional LinkedIn profile or company page URL. Romance preferences can reveal sensitive information about you.
  • Account activity: profile and book update times, participation, email notification and contact-sharing preferences, when you first reveal an introduction, records of introductions made through the service, and any compatibility or book-recommendation ratings you submit. Your match reviews are private feedback to Zebra Book Club and are not shown to the other member.
  • Friend matchmaking suggestions: the two Instagram handles you submit, your account identifier, and the submission time. These romance suggestions are private to the service operator; submitting a suggestion does not reveal whether either person has an account or create an introduction.
  • Merch referrals: your selected raffle prize, the Instagram handle you credit when creating a profile, the referring and referred account identifiers when a credit can be resolved, and the referral time and status. We use these records to track raffle entries and review duplicate or invalid credits. Referrers are not given access to the profiles or contact details of people they invite.
  • Communications: information you send when contacting us.
  • Technical information: our hosting and service providers may process IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and error or security logs to deliver and protect the service.

You choose a city from a catalog. The matchmaking platform does not request your precise device location or street address. Book and city suggestions are searched locally using catalogs hosted on our website or bundled in the iOS app. Typing a search does not send its text to our server or a book provider; the books and city you submit are saved with your profile.

Instagram is optional. You can sign up, receive matches, and remove your Instagram handle without losing eligibility. You can share your sign-in email with released matches alongside Instagram, share it only when Instagram isn’t shared, or keep it private. Earlier email permissions keep their original scope. Choose during signup or update your choice in Settings. Email sharing is separate from notifications about new matches. We do not ask for your Instagram password or connect to your Instagram account.

2. How we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, check the adult age requirement, save and display your profile, support introductions based on your preferences and books, calculate community totals, maintain your private reading library, review reports and enforce blocks, respond to requests, and protect the service from misuse.

Introductions appear when released to your account; creating a profile does not guarantee an introduction or a particular schedule. You can control match-email preferences in Settings. We may separately contact you about your account, a support request, or a service or security issue.

We do not sell your personal information or use your matchmaking profile for targeted advertising.

3. What other people can see

Your matches: when an introduction is released, the other person can see your chosen name, Instagram username and profile link if provided, age, city, region and country, favorite books, your current read and personal book recommendation if provided, and relevant friendship or business introduction answers. Instagram is shared with your matches for any connection type so they can reach you; it is not included in the community map or book totals. Your optional LinkedIn link is shared only with business matches. A released match can see both your email and Instagram when you choose to share them together. Your reading preferences from the signup checklist stay on your own profile. Turning off email contact hides it from future views, but cannot remove copies already made by a match. The introduction does not include your exact birthday or private romance preferences, including your ideal-match answer. Your reveal status is saved for your own account and is not shown to your match. Information you choose to put into a shared introduction answer will be visible with that answer.

The member dashboard: signed-in members can see the total number of completed profiles, counts by city at city-center locations, counts by country, and the most selected books overall and in each country. These totals contain no names, account identifiers, or links to individual profiles. They include paused profiles and may be reused from your browser or app cache for up to one hour when loading the dashboard.

The public community map: the home-page backdrop shows reader locations grouped by city. Its public data includes the completed-profile total, city-center coordinates, and counts per city, including paused profiles. It contains no names, account identifiers, contact details, book selections, or individual profile links. We publish a shared snapshot with the website approximately hourly, so profile changes and deletions are not reflected immediately. Publishing or service outages can delay updates; the site stops displaying snapshots older than three hours.

Service operation: authorized people operating Zebra Book Club and our service providers may access information as needed to run the service, provide support, investigate abuse, or comply with legal obligations. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights and safety.

Blocks: blocking a reader prevents new pairings and hides existing introductions between you from subsequent server requests. It cannot remove information already seen, cached, copied, or shared outside the service. Unblocking can make prior introductions visible again unless the other person has also blocked you.

Your profile is not a public member directory. We cannot control copies that a match makes of information already shared with them.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase provides authentication, the profile and matchmaking database, and server functions. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Apple provides native sign-in and maps in the iOS app. The app does not request your device location or send your matchmaking profile to Apple Maps. Map requests are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Google provides sign-in. Google privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the public website and may log requests to it. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Open Library, a project of the Internet Archive, supplies reference book metadata and some cover images. The current platform hosts its suggestion catalog and covers on our website or bundles them in the app, so browsing suggestions or displaying a cover does not contact Open Library. Older versions of the platform sent book searches or identifiers through our server to Open Library without your account identifier, email, or profile. Those search caches are separate from profiles and are not an account-linked search history. Internet Archive terms and privacy policy.

City data comes from GeoNames, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The world outline comes from Natural Earth (public domain). We have filtered the city catalog for search and projected the map for display. These files are hosted with our service; displaying them does not send your profile to GeoNames or Natural Earth. Book links may open Amazon with a Zebra Book Club affiliate tag and may earn us a commission. We do not attach your account identifier, email, or profile to those links. Amazon and other external services handle visits and purchases under their own policies. Links to social networks, podcast platforms, and other external websites are governed by those services’ policies when you visit them.

5. Storage and retention

Your browser stores a sign-in session and related authentication data so you can stay logged in. It also remembers your selected connection category and temporarily caches community aggregates and whether Google sign-in is available. The dashboard aggregate cache is associated with your signed-in account and cleared on sign-out; it contains no individual profiles, contacts, match details, or review ratings. Clearing browser storage removes these local values; it does not delete your account. We do not add our own advertising or analytics trackers to this site.

The iOS app keeps its sign-in session in the device Keychain. A protected local cache stores community aggregates for up to one hour, scoped to your account and cleared on sign-out or deletion. Private profiles, match details, and library ratings are not stored in that aggregate cache. Book descriptions and search catalogs are bundled public reference data. The app does not include advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

We keep your account and profile while you use the service. Deleting your account removes your authentication account, matchmaking profile, preferences, private library and ratings, match memberships, and associated live block/report records from the live database. Backups, security logs, and records needed for legal obligations or resolving disputes may remain for their applicable retention periods. Copies already received by another person are not removed by deleting your account.

If you follow a personal invitation, we temporarily store the inviter’s Instagram handle in your browser so it can be filled in after Google sign-in. An unfinished invitation expires after 30 days and is cleared when your profile is saved or you sign out. A prize selection may also be saved while you finish signing up. Personal invitation links include the inviter’s Instagram handle and are visible to anyone the link is shared with. Account deletion removes associated referral credits from the live database.

We use access controls to restrict profile access, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile from your dashboard or the app’s Profile tab. Your birthday is fixed after signup; contact us if it is incorrect. Favorite books and the current read have separate 24-hour edit limits. Finishing a current read saves its rating to your private library, clears the current read, and starts its next 24-hour window. Library ratings can be edited independently.
  • Pause matchmaking to stop eligibility for new introductions. Pausing does not delete your profile, remove it from community totals, or withdraw introductions already released.
  • Control match emails with the email preference in your dashboard.
  • Delete your account using Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app, or the dashboard’s “Delete my account” option. This deletes the shared Zebra account used by the website and app; it does not delete your Google or Apple account. You can also revoke Zebra Book Club’s access in your provider settings; revoking access alone does not delete your matchmaking profile. If we cannot automatically remove Apple sign-in permission during deletion, we show Apple’s instructions for removing it manually.
  • Contact us to request a copy of your information, correction or deletion, or help with privacy choices. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. Age requirement

Matchmaking is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow minors to create matchmaking profiles. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and remove the account as appropriate.

8. Updates and contact

We will update this policy when our practices change and revise the date above. For material changes, we will provide a notice on the site or through your account, and seek consent where required.
