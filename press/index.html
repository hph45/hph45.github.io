Zebra Book Club is a free book-based matchmaking platform for adults, with introductions for romance, friendship, and business connections.

The basics

Members choose one to three favorite books and set their preferences. Introductions are released in rounds. The website and iOS app share the same account.

3,294 active accounts as of November 23, 2026.

The app is listed by developer Henry Houghton. More about Zebra.
