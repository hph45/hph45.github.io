Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. If you do not agree, please do not create or use a matchmaking account.

1. The service

Zebra Book Club brings readers together through books, reviews, podcasts, and book-based matchmaking. Matchmaking lets you choose one or more connection types—romance, friends, and business partners—create a profile, and view community statistics and any introductions released to you.

The platform is in an early stage. Weekly introductions and match emails are coming soon; they are not currently running. Creating a profile does not guarantee an introduction, a particular schedule, compatibility, or any personal or business outcome.

The current matchmaking service is free. If we introduce paid features, we will explain their prices and terms before you choose to purchase them.

2. Your account

You must be at least 18 and legally able to agree to these terms to use matchmaking. Use your own Google account, provide accurate information, and keep your profile up to date. You are responsible for safeguarding access to your account and telling us if you suspect unauthorized use.

Choose one to three favorite books; only the first is required. Book selections can be changed once every rolling 24 hours, beginning when you create your profile or last change your picks. Other profile details can be edited without that waiting period.

Google sign-in confirms access to a Google account. It does not verify a person’s identity, age, background, qualifications, or profile claims.

3. Community conduct

Treat other members with respect and honor their boundaries. You must not:

  • Harass, threaten, stalk, discriminate against, or sexually exploit another person.
  • Impersonate anyone, misrepresent your age or identity, run scams, send spam, or use introductions for unwanted solicitation.
  • Share someone else’s personal information or private introduction without their permission.
  • Upload unlawful material, violate another person’s rights, or include information you do not have permission to share.
  • Scrape member information, bypass access controls or account limits, disrupt the service, or attempt unauthorized access.

Report concerns to henry@zebrabookclub.com. We may investigate reports and restrict access to protect the community.

Only submit information you are comfortable sharing as described in the Privacy Policy. Avoid putting confidential business material or contact information into an introduction unless you want a match to receive it. Other members may retain information already shared with them.

Zebra Book Club is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service.

7. Leaving or suspension

You can pause matchmaking or delete your account from your dashboard. Pausing stops eligibility for new introductions but leaves your profile in community totals and does not withdraw introductions already released. Deletion removes your live account and profile as explained in the Privacy Policy.
