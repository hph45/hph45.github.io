Support

For account help, questions, or safety concerns, email zebrabookclub@proton.me.

Sign in to your existing account

The app and website use the same Zebra Book Club account. If you joined on the website with Google, use that Google account in the app. To add Apple sign-in, first sign in to your existing account, then open Profile → Settings → Connect Apple.

Using Apple’s Hide My Email before linking Apple may create a different account. Contact us if your profile or matches seem to be missing; do not send us your passwords or verification codes.

Email login and password recovery

If you already have a Zebra Book Club password, choose Other sign-in options on the app’s welcome page, then enter your email and password. This logs in to an existing account; entering an email does not create a new one. New members can continue with Apple or Google.

Choose Forgot password? to request a reset link, then open it on the same device and app installation where you requested it. Choose a new password when the app opens. If the link expires or you change devices, request a new link. Your Zebra Book Club password is separate from your email provider’s password.

Your books

Matchmaking favorites and your current read have separate 24-hour change limits. In Currently reading, choose Finished reading to rate a book and move it into your library, or Switch book to choose another title. Finishing starts the next 24-hour current-read window. Private library ratings can be edited at any time and support quarter-star steps.

Profile and birthday

Edit your profile in the Profile tab. Your exact birthday is private and is fixed after signup; only your age is shown to matches. Contact us if your birthday is incorrect.

Report or block a reader

Open the introduction in Matches and choose Report reader or Block reader. Reports are private to Zebra Book Club. Blocking hides subsequent views of your introductions from each other and prevents future pairings. It cannot remove contact details already copied or shared outside the service. Manage blocks in Profile → Settings → Blocked readers.

For a concern outside an introduction, email zebrabookclub@proton.me. Include enough context for us to investigate, but do not send passwords or unnecessary private information.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. On the website, use Delete my account in your dashboard. Deletion removes your shared Zebra Book Club account, profile, library, ratings, and match memberships; it does not delete your Apple or Google account. If Apple access cannot be removed automatically, the app links to Apple’s instructions after deletion.
