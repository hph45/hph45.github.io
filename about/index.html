How Zebra works

Zebra Book Club matches people through their favorite books, for romance, friendship, and business connections.

Start with your books.

Pick one to three favorites. We look for shared books, authors, and reading tastes, alongside your connection preferences.

Choose who you want to meet.

Select romance, friendship, business partners, or a combination. Set a distance preference, or choose matches anywhere.

Check your introductions.

Matches are released in rounds. Sign in to see yours, their shared contact details, and any book recommendation. You decide whether to reach out. A match in every round is not guaranteed.

Free. For readers 18+.

Join from around the world on the web or iOS. Both use the same account. You can pause matching in your profile.
