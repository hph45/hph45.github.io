## Collect one or two member stories

No stories, quotes, or permission have been collected yet. The press screenshot
shows explicitly fictional app-review profiles; it is not evidence of an outcome.
Keep interview notes and consent outside the public site and repository. Only
add a story to the public pack after the person approves the exact material.

Draft invitation, for the owner to send to a member who has volunteered feedback:

> Would you be open to sharing what happened after your Zebra introduction?
> No pressure, and it won’t affect your matches. We’d like to hear which book
> started the conversation, whether you spoke or met, and what worked or didn’t.
> We won’t publish your words or share your contact details with a reporter
> without asking you first.

Ask which book prompted the conversation, what they actually did next, what
connection type it was, and whether their expectations changed. Distinguish an
introduction, an exchange of messages, a meeting, and an ongoing relationship.
Do not turn a brief exchange into a success story.

Before use, record approval for the exact quote/story, public name or pseudonym,
photo if any, publication channels, and separate permission for any reporter
introduction. Do not publish the other match's identity or messages without
their permission. Send the final excerpt for factual review and record its
approval date. Choose the final story based on what members actually report.
